COURSE: Operating Systems (Semester 4)

MODULE 1: CPU SCHEDULING
- FCFS (First-Come, First-Served): Non-preemptive. High average waiting time (Convoy effect).
- SJF (Shortest Job First): Optimal average waiting time. Can cause starvation for long processes.
- Round Robin (RR): Preemptive using time quantum (e.g., 2ms-5ms). High context-switching overhead if quantum is too small.

MODULE 2: DEADLOCKS
- Four Necessary Conditions: Mutual Exclusion, Hold and Wait, No Preemption, Circular Wait.
- Banker's Algorithm: Used for deadlock avoidance by verifying if the system remains in a safe state.

EXAM RULES:
- OS Oral Exam focuses on Process Lifecycle, Page Replacement (LRU/FIFO), and System Calls.

SUBJECT: Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning

KEY FORMULAS & CONCEPTS:
- Mean Squared Error (MSE): Loss = (1/n) * sum((y_actual - y_predicted)^2)
- Precision: TP / (TP + FP)
- Recall: TP / (TP + FN)
- F1-Score: 2 * (Precision * Recall) / (Precision + Recall)

MODEL CHEATSHEET:
- Overfitting: High training accuracy, low test accuracy. Fix: Regularization (L1/L2), Dropout, or adding more training data.
- Underfitting: Low training and low test accuracy. Fix: Increase model complexity or add features.
